# Mivvo Interpret

**Clear Medical Lab Insights**

Mivvo Interpret helps patients and clinicians understand common laboratory and vital sign results in simple, clear language.

---

### 🔗 Live Demo
[https://marv48531-dotcom.github.io/mivvo-interpret/](https://marv48531-dotcom.github.io/mivvo-interpret/)

---

### Features

- **Patient Mode** – Simple explanations + questions to ask your doctor
- **Clinician Mode** – Quick reference + EHR-style summary notes
- Supports:
  - Blood Pressure
  - Hemoglobin (g/dL & % Sahli)
  - Fasting Blood Glucose
  - Total Cholesterol
  - Serum Creatinine
- English & German language support
- Progressive Web App (can be installed on mobile)
- Educational articles for better health literacy

---

### 3-Part Output Framework

1. **Status Baseline** – Objective statement of the result
2. **Patient Context** – Everyday language explanation
3. **Clinical Partnership Prompt** – Helpful questions for the doctor

---

### How to Use

1. Open the app
2. Choose Patient or Clinician mode
3. Select a test
4. Enter the values
5. Get a clear interpretation
